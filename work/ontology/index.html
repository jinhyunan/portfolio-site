02 / Structure — TYPES / RELATIONS

Relationships need
names, too.

Systems, equipment, properties, methods and evidence are different types. Required inputs and reference sources also connect through different relationships.

I implemented concept types, a relationship schema and a graph explorer. General concepts are distinguished from individual pieces of equipment.
