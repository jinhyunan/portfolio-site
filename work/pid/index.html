01 / Problem — DRAWING / CONTEXT

The lines connect.
Does the meaning follow?

Equipment and piping connect on a drawing. But the reasons for a change and the conditions of a calculation do not always travel with those lines.

The starting point

I needed a workflow that could handle both the drawing and its engineering meaning.

02 / Structure — CONNECT / STRUCTURE

Equipment. Ports. Links.
A grammar for design.

Give equipment and ports identifiers, then structure their connections. An object selected on the drawing remains the same object in calculation and review.

Bring the result
back to the drawing.

Link results and open conditions to their equipment and piping. When comparing candidates, keep both the changes and the unresolved questions visible.

Supporting judgment

05 / What remains — CONTEXT / NEXT QUESTION

A useful tool leaves
the next question visible.

I developed a workbench for drawing structure and generation, calculation links and change review. Public and synthetic cases help verify the connections as the scope develops.

My role and current scope

I defined the data structure and review workflow from a power-plant engineering perspective, then implemented it with AI. Actual design use requires project conditions and review and approval by the responsible engineer.
